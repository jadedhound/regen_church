When should I come?

We have 3 services every Sunday, choose the one that fits you best:

  • Morning Service: 10am. A welcoming start to the day with a kids’ program.
  • Mandarin Service: 2pm. Feel at home worshiping in your native language.
  • Night Service: 5pm. End the day on a high note, great for university students and young adults.

118 Wellington Rd
Clayton VIC 3168

What happens at church?

Before the service

There’s plenty of on-site parking. Our Hospitality Team will greet you at the entrance and help with anything you need. Don’t worry if you’re running late—they’ll make sure you find a seat as soon as you arrive.

{{ img_macro::standalone( image="homepage_before_service", sizes="(min-width: 1120px) 480px, (min-width: 780px) 43.13vw, calc(100vw - 64px)", alt="Before service", class="rounded-md my-auto md:order-7", ) }}

During the service

We sing to God, pray collectively, read the Bible, and hear a sermon from one of our pastors. Every other week we also partake in communion. Don't worry if any of this sounds odd or unfamiliar; come as you are and participate as much or as little as you feel comfortable.

{{ img_macro::standalone( image="homepage_during_service", sizes="(min-width: 1120px) 480px, (min-width: 780px) 43.13vw, calc(100vw - 64px)", alt="During service", class="rounded-md my-auto md:order-8", ) }}

Kids and families

Children start the service with the adults. After singing songs to God, they hear an engaging kids' talk before they head off to programs tailored for them. Feel free to accompany your child to their program or to remain with the congregation.

{{ img_macro::standalone( image="homepage_kids", sizes="(min-width: 1120px) 480px, (min-width: 780px) 43.13vw, calc(100vw - 64px)", alt="Kids and families during service", class="rounded-md my-auto md:order-11", ) }}

After the service

After the service, hang around for a delicous cup of barista-style coffee and some light morning snacks. This is our gift to you and is completely free.
