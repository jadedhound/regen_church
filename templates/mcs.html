What are MCs?

Missional Communities (MCs) are small groups committed to being formed by Jesus in community for the sake of others. We usually meet weekly in homes where we eat together, pray and study the Bible and seek to serve one another and the community at large.

The best way to see what MCs are all about is to join one and see what it's like.

How can I join?

You have 3 ways to join: fill out the newcomer form, speak with a pastor, or connect directly with the leader of the MC you want to join.
